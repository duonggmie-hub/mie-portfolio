A little more about me

Hi, I’m Mie — a Toronto-based graphic designer and Advertising & Graphic Design graduate from Humber Polytechnic. Creativity has always been part of who I am, from journaling and crafting to my previous experience as a nail technician. Those hands-on experiences shaped my attention to detail, colour, composition, and storytelling.

With experience in marketing and design, I have contributed to real estate campaigns, created digital content, and developed visual identities that connect with people. Today, I combine strategy, emotion, and visual communication to create work that is both beautiful and purposeful.
